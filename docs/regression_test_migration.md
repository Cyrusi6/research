## M1.1.2 RouteOutcome authority and hermetic audit

- `tests/test_s3_precommit_orchestration.py::test_orchestrator_prefers_repair_route_over_blocked_result_status`, `...pause...`, and `...integrity...` used a monkeypatched `ExperimentAgent` route plus a synthetic Gate PASS.
